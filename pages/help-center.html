Help Center

How do I sell a book?

Go to Sell Books or Upload Book from the Home screen, fill in the title, author, category, ISBN, and price, then submit. It appears in Recent Uploads immediately.

How do I track my order?

Open Profile → My Orders to see the status of every order: Processing, Shipped, Delivered, or Cancelled.

How do I edit or remove a listing?

Open Profile → My Uploaded Books. Each listing has Edit and Delete actions.

How do I mark a book as sold?

From My Uploaded Books, tap Mark Sold and enter the final price — it will then show up under My Sales with your earnings.

How do I change my password?

Open Profile → Change Password and enter your current and new password. If you're signed out, use Forgot Password on the sign-in screen instead.

Can I use EduBook in Telugu?

Yes — Profile → Language currently switches the Profile section between English and Telugu.
