Privacy Policy

What we collect

Your username, email, and a securely hashed password are stored to run your account. Profile details like phone number, date of birth, and photo are stored locally on your device unless you sync them to your account.

How we use it

To sign you in, show your orders and listings, and let you reset a forgotten password via a one-time code sent to your email.

What we don't do

  • We never store passwords in plain text.
  • We don't share your email or phone number with other users or third parties for marketing.

Your choices

You can edit or delete your profile information at any time, download a copy of your data, or permanently delete your account from Profile → Privacy & Security.
